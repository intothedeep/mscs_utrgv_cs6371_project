2.1 Experiment frame and algorithm draft (inference)

  1. loop functions split and merge the target group on a fixed schedule. The targets are 8 to 16 foot-bots.
  2. Each target sends "I am a target" with the range-and-bearing sensor [S-SMT-10]. The tracking foot-bots number 12 to 30.
  3. A tracking robot clusters the sensed targets by distance. A gap larger than d means a different group.
  4. It joins the nearest group. When two groups are farther apart than d, the robots on the far side follow the far group.
  5. The side with more robots per target hands robots to the side with fewer. A robot moves only if the imbalance lasts T seconds (hysteresis time T).
  6. Each sub-swarm builds a ring around its center. When two centers come closer than d, the rings merge into one.

Open problems that remain (inference, based on the abstracts and the first pages of the papers):

  1. Local proportional reallocation when a target group repeatedly splits and merges. The tracking side splits by individual target [S-RR-12], [S-RR-25].
  2. The trade-off curve of observation ratio and switch frequency. Switch counts are measured [S-RR-12]. No source was found that plots a curve by varying the hysteresis time.
  3. Resizing the formation when robots move away. Encirclement thickness follows target strength [S-RR-1]. Resizing to a change in robot count is not in the abstract.
  4. Handing over from tracking to herding. The ratio of observing roles to herding roles. A division of search and tracking exists [S-RR-15].
  5. Splitting the shepherds again in proportion to group size when a sub-group splits again during herding. Recent learned methods pick one target individual [S-RR-3], [S-RR-16]. Ordering the collection of groups exists for one shepherd [S-SMT-27].
  6. Field herding of cattle with several robots. No peer-reviewed study was found [S-RR-14], [S-RR-30], [S-RR-31].
  7. Multi-robot herding with animal welfare constraints. Only single-drone studies exist [S-SMT-28]. Socially aware safe-distance encirclement exists [S-RR-17].
  8. A microswarm tracking several cell groups at the same time. Today one swarm goes in turn [S-RR-13]. Splitting by field parameters works [S-RR-28].
  9. Most evaluation is in simulation. Real tests are a minority [S-RR-1], [S-RR-11], [S-RR-15].
